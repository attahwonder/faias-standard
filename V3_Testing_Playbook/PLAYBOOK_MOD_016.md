# TP-MOD-016 — Evaluation-to-Deployment External Validity: Divergence Inventory, Trace-Rate and Paired-Framing Testing

**Procedure ID:** TP-MOD-016
**IRQ:** IRQ-MOD-016 — Pre-Deployment Evaluation Evidence Without Established External Validity Against Deployment Conditions
**Assurance objective:** AO-MOD-016 — where the institution relies on its own pre-deployment testing, UAT, pilot or vendor evaluation results to support an authorisation decision, that evidence shall carry a documented and evidenced basis for external validity against deployment conditions: the respects in which the evaluation environment diverges from the production context shall be identified; the rate at which the system's own traces indicate it is reasoning about being tested, graded or monitored shall be measured on the institution's own traces where those traces exist; the consequential result shall be reported split by that indication; and the authorisation record shall state what the pre-deployment evidence can and cannot carry. The reviewer examines the evaluation-environment record and the firm's own pilot and staging evidence; the reviewer runs nothing in production and initiates no production traffic.
**★ Confidence:** ★2 (carried from canvas — see canvas §11. Summary: research exists and the method is bespoke, with professional judgement dominant. Published methods exist for measuring evaluation awareness — a classification benchmark (Needham et al., 2025) and a representation study (Heidari et al., 2026), both arXiv preprints with no peer-reviewed venue recorded — but neither addresses the deployer's external-validity question, neither yields a threshold or pass criterion, the most cited methods need trace, activation or weight access a firm consuming a hosted model does not hold, no financial-services application was located, and no settled financial-services benchmark exists. Rating set by the owner on 11 September 2026.)
**Method classification:** Emerging Practice (No Established Benchmark) / bespoke, informed by the evaluation-awareness measurement literature cited in the canvas (Needham et al., 2025, arXiv:2505.23836; Heidari et al., 2026, arXiv:2608.21766) and by the external-validity demonstration method articulated in the EU General-Purpose AI Code of Practice — Safety and Security Chapter (Definitions, 'external validity'; Appendix 3.1), **adapted from a provider obligation on Article 55 Signatories to the deployer's own evidence, an adaptation the canvas states rather than implies**. The canvas records plainly that **no FAIAS-standard acceptance threshold exists for any measure in this procedure**, that the published methods supply constructs and measurement approaches rather than pass criteria, and that the procedure **bounds divergence from above and cannot establish its absence**.

## Preconditions

- **Environment:** non-production. Limbs run against the firm's existing evaluation records and against a staging replica of the deployed pipeline. **No limb contacts production, generates a production request, or involves a live counterparty.** Where a limb compares evaluation inputs against production inputs, it uses records and distributional summaries the firm already holds; the reviewer reads them and initiates nothing.
- **Access needed:** the authorisation paper and the specific evidence it cites; the evaluation-environment record or, where none exists, the harness configuration and the people who built it; the firm's own pilot and staging evidence for this system; retained reasoning traces from the evaluation runs, where the system emits and retains them, and the scoring method and judge identity and version if the firm has already scored them; a staging replica of the deployed pipeline able to run the firm's own task set at a pinned configuration under two framings; existing distributional summaries of production prompts and inputs; the provider's published safety-evaluation and evaluation-awareness statements for the model version in use; any post-deployment comparison of realised against pre-deployment metric.
- **Artefacts requested in advance (3 weeks — locating the evaluation-environment record, and establishing whether reasoning traces were retained at all, is the long pole):** the authorisation paper with the evidence cited in it; the evaluation-environment documentation; the harness configuration including whether any prompt or scaffold announces a test; the evaluation trace store and its retention policy; the firm's documented tolerances for any of the three measures, or confirmation that none exist; the inventory of decisions and reports that rest on this evidence; the provider's statements for the deployed version; the post-deployment comparison where one exists.
- **Personnel:** reviewer (lead); the team that built the evaluation harness; the team that sought authorisation; the independent validation function; the platform owner for the staging replica; the vendor-management contact for the provider's published statements.
- **Shared asset:** the staging replica used across the MOD domain. This procedure needs the replica to run the firm's own task set twice under two framings at a pinned configuration, so the determinism discipline stated in the domain overview applies — configuration recorded and held fixed, fixed repetition count where the endpoint is non-deterministic, variance reported.
- **Dependency:** run alongside **TP-MOD-011** and **TP-MOD-015** where they are in scope. The three together are the evaluation-evidence-integrity set: MOD-011 asks what the evidence contains, MOD-015 asks who scored it, and this procedure asks what setting produced it. Where the firm scores its own traces with a language model, **the judge's validity is TP-MOD-015's finding and is not re-derived here**; this procedure records the judge's identity and pinned version and cross-refers.

## Test design

- **Limb 1 — divergence inventory.** The evaluation-environment record for the cited evidence is reviewed and, with the firm, an explicit inventory is constructed of the respects in which the environment diverges from the production context: data realism, counterparty simulation, tool and scaffold parity, human-message provenance, volume, time horizon, and whether the prompt or scaffold announces that a test is under way. Each divergence is recorded together with whether the authorisation record acknowledges it. **Where no evaluation-environment record exists, that absence is the limb's primary finding and the inventory is constructed from scratch as a reviewer work product, clearly labelled as such** — a reviewer-constructed inventory is not evidence that the firm held one.
- **Limb 2 — evidence-population review.** The firm's own pilot and staging evidence is sampled, and for each sampled item the reviewer establishes what population the result was measured on and what the approval paper claims it shows. **The reviewer reads this evidence; the reviewer runs nothing in production.** Where a pilot is the realism anchor for the firm's argument, the reviewer reads the pilot's own records; it does not extend, rerun or instrument the pilot.
- **Limb 3 — trace-rate measurement.** Where the system's logs carry reasoning traces, a recorded sample of the firm's own evaluation traces is scored for indications that the system is reasoning about being tested, graded or monitored, and the rate is reported with the scoring method and any judge named and version-pinned. **Where the deployed model emits no retained traces, the limb is recorded as not measurable on this system and the finding says so; no proxy is substituted and no rate from a provider's publication about a different model version is carried into the firm's result as though it were the firm's measurement.**
- **Limb 4 — awareness-stratified reporting.** The consequential metric is reported on the subset where the indication is present and on the subset where it is absent, so the homogeneity of the evidence base is visible. **No FAIAS tolerance is set for the gap**; where the firm has documented one, the comparison is made against it, and where it has not, the measurement stands and the missing tolerance is reported as a separate finding.
- **Limb 5 — paired-framing runs.** The firm's own task set is run in the staging replica under two framings — one test-framed, one deployment-framed — with identical task content and the configuration pinned and recorded, and the delta on the consequential metric is reported. The delta is an **upper bound on divergence on that task set**, not a clearance, and is reported in those terms.
- **Limb 6 — distributional divergence analysis.** The distribution of evaluation prompts and inputs is compared against the distribution of production prompts and inputs using records and summaries the firm already holds, and the respects in which they differ are reported. **No live system is contacted and no production request is generated by the reviewer.**
- **Limb 7 — provider-statement reconciliation and post-deployment read.** The provider's published evaluation-awareness statements for the model version in use are reconciled against the firm's own results, or their absence for that version is recorded; and the firm's post-deployment comparison of realised against pre-deployment metric, where one exists, is read together with its disposition of any divergence.
- **Measurement-floor discipline (applies to limbs 3 and 4).** A verbalised trace rate is a **lower bound**, not an estimate. The canvas's cited basis for this is Heidari et al. (2026): representations of evaluation awareness are linearly decodable from residual streams while aligning only in part with verbalisation. The reviewer reports the measured rate as a floor and does not describe a low rate as evidence of absence. The activation-level method that produced that finding requires white-box access and **is not run in this procedure**; where the firm runs open-weight models with activation access and has run such an analysis itself, its results are read as L3 evidence.
- **Threshold discipline.** FAIAS prescribes no threshold for the trace rate, the awareness-stratified gap or the framing delta, because none is established in the field; the canvas states this rather than implying it. Where the firm has set none, the measurements stand and the missing threshold is reported separately. **The reviewer does not silently substitute one: where the firm has set none, the measurement is reported, the absence is a finding, and any tolerance the reviewer applies by judgement is stated explicitly with its basis.**
- **Benign-payload principles:** not applicable. The governing safety rules here are the non-production rule — every limb runs on records the firm holds or in staging, and no limb generates production traffic or touches a live counterparty — and the labelling rule that distinguishes a firm-held artefact from a reviewer work product.

## Steps

1. Identify the authorisation decision in scope and the specific pre-deployment evidence its paper cites. **Record:** the decision, the evidence objects, and the inventory of downstream conclusions resting on them — which bounds the consequence of every adverse finding below.
2. Obtain the evaluation-environment record for that evidence. **Record:** the record, or the fact that none exists.
3. Construct the divergence inventory against the production context across the seven named dimensions, and establish for each divergence whether the authorisation record acknowledges it. **Record:** the inventory, its source (firm-held or reviewer-constructed, labelled), and the acknowledgement status per item.
4. Establish what the authorisation record states about what the evidence can and cannot carry. **Record:** the statement verbatim, or its absence, and whether any stated limit is supported by the evidence.
5. Sample the firm's own pilot and staging evidence; for each sampled item establish the measured population and the claim made from it. **Record:** the sample, the population per item, and any item where the claim exceeds the population.
6. Establish whether the system emits and retains reasoning traces, and whether the firm has scored them. **Record:** the retention position and any existing scoring with its method and pinned judge version; where no traces are retained, record that limbs 3 and 4 are not measurable on this system and stop those limbs here.
7. Score a recorded sample of evaluation traces for test-awareness indication, with the scoring method and judge version fixed and recorded before scoring begins. **Record:** the rate, the method, the judge and version, the sample and its selection basis — and the rate's status as a lower bound.
8. Report the consequential metric on the indicated and non-indicated subsets. **Record:** both values, the subset sizes, and the gap.
9. Obtain the firm's documented tolerances for the trace rate, the stratified gap and the framing delta. **Record:** the tolerances and their approval route, or the fact that none exist.
10. Fix and record the paired-framing design — task set, the two framings, the pinned configuration, the repetition count — before any run. **Record:** the design, fixed in advance.
11. Run the paired-framing limb in the staging replica and report the delta on the consequential metric, with run-to-run variance. **Record:** per-framing results, the delta, the variance, and the configuration.
12. Run the distributional divergence analysis over records the firm already holds. **Record:** the respects in which evaluation and production input distributions differ, and the basis of each comparison.
13. Reconcile the provider's published evaluation-awareness statements for the deployed model version against the firm's own results. **Record:** the reconciliation, or the absence of provider statements for that version.
14. Read the firm's post-deployment comparison of realised against pre-deployment metric and its disposition of any divergence. **Record:** the comparison and disposition, or its absence.
15. Review independent-review coverage: was the external-validity argument assessed by a function independent of the team that built the harness and of the team seeking authorisation? **Record:** the report, or its absence.

## Interpretation

- **Pass** — an evaluation-environment record exists and names the divergences from production; the authorisation record acknowledges them and states what the evidence can and cannot carry; where reasoning traces are retained, the test-awareness indication has been measured on the firm's own traces with the scoring method and judge version pinned, and the consequential metric is reported split by that indication and dispositioned against a documented tolerance, or, where none exists, against a clearly stated judged tolerance with the absence reported as a finding; where no reasoning traces are retained, that limitation is recorded in the authorisation record rather than left unstated, and the finding states what the unavailable split narrows; the provider's published evaluation-awareness statements for the model version in use have been reconciled against the firm's own results, or their absence for that version recorded; paired test-framed and deployment-framed runs have been performed at a pinned configuration with the delta recorded as an upper bound; independent review of the external-validity argument has been performed; and a post-deployment comparison exists with a recorded disposition.
- **Fail** — the authorisation paper asserts that UAT, internal evaluation or pilot results are representative of deployment with no stated basis and no evaluation-environment record to supply one; **or** no divergence inventory exists; **or** traces are retained but have never been scored, or a provider's rate for a different model version is presented as the firm's own measurement; **or** the consequential metric is a single figure with no stratified split and no statement that the split was impossible and why; **or** no paired-framing comparison has been performed and the firm's position is that the harness is production-representative because it was built to be; **or** the authorisation record states no limit, or states a limit the evidence does not support such as divergence having been ruled out; **or** a post-deployment divergence was attributed to drift, data quality or a model change without the evidence base being reconsidered.
- **Partial** — the canvas's own shape: an external-validity basis exists for one evaluation use case while the same authorisation also relies on other pilot or vendor evidence with no equivalent basis; the objective is not met for the second pending equivalent treatment. **The distinct partial case this procedure must report precisely:** where the deployed model retains no reasoning traces and the authorisation record does not record that limitation, limbs 3 and 4 are unavailable on that system and the narrowing goes unstated. The unavailability is a limitation of the evidence available, not a failure of the firm's control; not recording it, and not saying what it narrows, is the firm's omission, and that omission is what makes the case Partial. Where the limitation and what it narrows are both recorded, a system retaining no traces is not held Partial on that ground. Report which downstream conclusions rest on which.

## Common pitfalls & limitations

- **Treating the evaluation result as self-evidently about deployment.** This is the canvas's blind-spot finding. The result is not in question; what it is evidence of is. A clean pass looks identical whether or not it bears on production behaviour.
- **Building the divergence inventory and then crediting it to the firm.** A reviewer-constructed inventory is a reviewer work product. Label it, and report the absence of a firm-held record as the finding it is.
- **Reading a low trace rate as absence.** The canvas's cited basis is explicit: representations of evaluation awareness align only in part with verbalisation. The measured rate is a floor. Reporting it as an estimate overstates the evidence in the firm's favour and is the error most likely to survive review unchallenged.
- **Importing a provider's rate as the firm's measurement.** A publisher's figure concerns a model version, an effort setting and a harness that are not the firm's. Reconcile it under limb 7; never substitute it for limb 3.
- **Extending or instrumenting the pilot.** The reviewer reads pilot evidence the firm generated for its own purposes. Running anything in production, or asking the firm to extend a pilot for the review, breaches the non-production precondition — and it is the breach the owner checks first.
- **Letting the paired-framing delta be read as a clearance.** The delta bounds divergence from above on one task set. A small delta is not evidence that divergence is absent, and the reporting language must not permit that reading.
- **Running the paired-framing limb without fixing the design first.** Framing, task set, configuration and repetition count are fixed and recorded before the first run, or the delta is attributable to the reviewer's choices.
- **Reporting a delta smaller than the run-to-run variance.** Where variance approaches the delta, the delta is uninterpretable. Report both, as the domain's determinism discipline requires.
- **Re-deriving judge validity here.** Where a language model scores the traces, its validity is TP-MOD-015's finding. Record the judge and its pinned version, cross-refer, and do not duplicate.
- **Confusing this with contamination or with shift.** TP-MOD-011 asks what the evidence contains; TP-MOD-010 asks about training-to-deployment shift. This asks about the setting that produced the evidence. A reviewer who collapses the three will report one finding where there are three questions.
- **Inventing an acceptance threshold.** The published methods supply constructs and measurement approaches, not bars. Where the firm has set none, report the measurements and the missing threshold separately.
- **Canvas limitation carried forward:** published methods exist for measuring evaluation awareness, but none addresses the deployer's external-validity question, none yields a threshold or pass criterion, the most cited methods need access a firm consuming a hosted model does not hold, and no financial-services application was located. ★2 — test design, threshold-setting and materiality are reviewer professional judgement, and the report must say so. **The procedure bounds divergence and cannot establish its absence; reporting that claims otherwise misstates the method.**
- **Consequence bounding.** Where an adverse finding is made, the affected population is every decision and report resting on the evidence base, not only the authorisation under review. Step 1 exists so the report can say how far the finding reaches.

**5 reviewer-days** (0.5 scoping the authorisation decision, the cited evidence and the downstream-conclusion inventory; 1 divergence inventory across the seven dimensions with the harness team; 0.5 pilot and staging evidence sampling and population review; 1 trace-scoring design, execution and awareness-stratified reporting, or the not-measurable determination and its consequences; 1 paired-framing design and execution in the staging replica including variance reporting; 0.5 distributional divergence analysis, provider reconciliation and post-deployment read; 0.5 reporting). Staging-replica engineering support and any judge-scoring compute are costed separately as non-reviewer support.
