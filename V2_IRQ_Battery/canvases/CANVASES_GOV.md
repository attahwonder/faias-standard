# FAIAS — Review Canvases: GOV (Governance) Domain

Sixteen Independent Review Questions covering IRQ-GOV-001 to IRQ-GOV-016, subdomains I.1 Inventory & Ownership, I.2 Approval, Tiering & Policy, I.3 Validation & Oversight, I.4 Documentation, Records & Regulatory. IRQ-GOV-013 to IRQ-GOV-016 were added in the v1.0.3 batch (1 August 2026); all four are ★2. Scope per TAXONOMY.md: whether the firm's governance structures — ownership, approval, tiering, independent validation, documentation, regulatory mapping and envelope control — actually operate as designed for AI systems, not whether a policy document exists. Governance testing is predominantly documentation and reconciliation review (established audit practice extended to an AI-specific object), so most Independent Tests in this file rate ★3 (credible audit-style methods, no AI-specific benchmark) or ★2 (bespoke, professional-judgement-dominant, e.g. inventory-completeness discovery); none is rated ★4/★5, and this is stated honestly rather than inflated. UK spelling throughout. All controls trace to a cited regulation, standard or peer-reviewed source; where no precedent exists, the control is explicitly marked "Emerging Practice."

Boundary note (TAXONOMY.md rulings): monitoring/alerting *capability* is homed in RES G.3 — GOV I.3 (IRQ-GOV-008) covers only whether operational signals reach accountable governance bodies and trigger revalidation. Independent *validation methods* (e.g. test design quality) are exercised in MOD/AGT/SEC/DAT canvases — GOV I.3 (IRQ-GOV-007) covers only whether validation is structurally independent and empowered to reject. *Human accountability* for individual decisions is homed in HUM D.4 (IRQ-HUM-006/-008) — GOV I.1 (IRQ-GOV-001) covers only whether a named, SMCR-mapped owner exists for the system as an asset. GOV IRQs below interrogate the governance-structure surface only and cross-link to RES/MOD/AGT/SEC/DAT/HUM where the boundary is shared.

IRQ-GOV-012 (operating-envelope drift) is a flagship IRQ and is drafted as the strongest canvas in this file, per ASSURANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md's Layer 4 Safety Case (Operating Envelope Declaration, Envelope Drift as Named Concept) and ENGAGEMENT_MODEL.md's Major-Change Re-Assessment Triggers.
