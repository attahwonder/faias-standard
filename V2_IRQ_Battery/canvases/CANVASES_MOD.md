## Domain Preamble

The MOD domain covers intrinsic behavioural properties of the model itself — capability boundaries, hallucination, calibration, specification gaming/reasoning integrity, distribution shift/evaluation integrity, model updates/configuration, and output stability & determinism (subdomains B.1–B.7). FS framing throughout: lending, underwriting, AML, claims, advice.
