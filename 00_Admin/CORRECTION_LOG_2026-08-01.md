## 1. SS1/23 text-content misattribution (SOURCE_REGISTER_REGULATORY.md)

The register entry asserted the five SS1/23 principles "explicitly extend to AI/ML models". Verified against both versions of the instrument: **the supervisory statement's own text — May 2023 and April 2026 alike — contains no occurrence of "artificial intelligence", "machine learning" or "AI".** The AI/ML relevance sentence in circulation is Bank of England landing-page summary text. A corpus-wide sweep found no other citation attributing AI/ML wording to SS1/23's text.

All four occurrences removed: the regulatory-alignment example table is now expressly illustrative with instruments to be verified per engagement, and prose references now point at SYSC generally and PRA SS1/23.

## 4. Internal-programme vocabulary removed from the standard's text

Working references to internal programme documents, decision identifiers and personal names that entered canvas text during drafting were removed or neutralised. The standard's text now carries no internal document references; provenance detail lives in the working records outside the manifest.
